The Beachfront Retreat

Where the River Meets the Arabian Sea

Nestled between the historic Moti Daman Fort, a tranquil river, and the vast Arabian Sea, The Fern Seaside Luxurious Tent Resort offers a rare coastal escape. Located directly across Jampore Beach, this premium destination features 84 total accommodations, including 37 luxury glamping tents designed for refined nature immersion.

Operational in selected optimal seasons, our premium tents provide high-speed Wi-Fi, elegant eco-friendly amenities, and private beachfront access, delivering a perfect staycation for families and couples seeking coastal tranquility.

Nearby Local Attractions

Discover historic forts, serene temples, and coastal viewpoints located just a short distance from our luxurious tent gates.

Moti Daman Fort

A 16th-century Portuguese fortress featuring high stone ramparts, historic cannons, and stunning views of the Daman Ganga River delta.

1.2 KM Away

Historic Jain Temple

Famous for its beautiful 18th-century glass murals, murals depicting Mahavira's life, and premium marble architecture.

2.5 KM Away

Satmalia Wildlife Sanctuary

A nearby nature reserve housing blackbucks, spotted deer, antelopes, and rare migratory waterbird species.

12.0 KM Away
