Nearby Attractions

Conveniently situated with seamless access to Borivali's historical sites, parks, beaches, and travel hubs.

Borivali Railway Station

One of Mumbai's primary railway hubs, providing fast trains and local connections directly to South Mumbai, Bandra, and corporate hotspots.

🚗 1.5 km

Sanjay Gandhi National Park

A vast protected forest reserve right in Mumbai, boasting pristine nature paths, tiger and lion safaris, and beautiful ancient rock-cut architecture.

Get Directions ↗
🚗 2.2 km

Mandapeshwar Caves

8th-century rock-cut shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva. An archaeological masterpiece showcasing remarkable pillars and rock sculptures.

Get Directions ↗
🚗 6.0 km

Kanheri Caves

A network of 109 Buddhist rock caves dating from the 1st century BC to 10th century AD, nestled deep within the green reserves of the national park.

🚗 8.5 km

Gorai Beach

A quiet, palm-lined beach on the outskirts of Mumbai. Highly popular for sunset walks, sea views, and accessing the nearby Global Pagoda temple.
