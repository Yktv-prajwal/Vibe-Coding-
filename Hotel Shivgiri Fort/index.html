THE SHIVGIRI EXPERIENCE

Where Serenity Meets Luxury

Tucked away in the tranquil landscape of Khanvel, Silvassa, SHIVGIRI RESORT offers a seamless integration of pristine nature and luxury hospitality. Surrounded by towering trees, manicured gardens, and the gentle breeze of the region, our resort is designed to be your private sanctuary.

Whether you are planning a relaxing weekend getaway, seeking a rejuvenating spa experience, or hosting a grand celebration, our world-class amenities and deeply personalized service ensure your stay is nothing short of exceptional.

Swimming Pool

Bask in the warm tropical sun by our outdoor pool. Take a refreshing swim in the azure waters or enjoy a quiet reading hour on the poolside loungers, while your little ones play safely in the shallow baby pool.

03 PRIVATE SANCTUARY

Luxury Rooms

Withdraw into our elegantly styled suites featuring private balconies, central air conditioning, smart televisions, and direct views of Shelti Lake. The perfect blend of rustic nature and plush comfort.

Dining at Dhara

Savour delicious multi-cuisine menus prepared by our executive chefs. From global Continental classics to rich Tandoori platters and authentic regional tribal recipes, every meal is a sensory celebration.

05 MILESTONES

Grand Event Celebrations

Host destination weddings, corporate training events, or family banquets. With our air-conditioned Kailasha Banquet Hall and 1-acre landscaped open party lawns, we turn your special milestones into timeless memories.

15 km

A stunning, expansive lake created by the Damanganga reservoir. Known for thrilling water sports, boating, and scenic sunset views.

Khanvel Tiger Safari

2 km
