The Story of White Feather

Where Wilderness Embraces Uncompromised Sophistication

Situated in the mystical landscape of Kauncha, Silvassa, right beside the majestic Dudhni Lake, lies a sanctuary that redefines glamping. We welcome you to a space where tall trees, calm waters, and luxury meet.

Our philosophy is centered on slow travel and deep connection. Walk on pathways lined with wild lilies, dip into our emerald infinity pool that blends visually into the reservoir, and dine in a restaurant framing the sunset. Each moment at White Feather is crafted to offer guests an immersive luxury escape from the ordinary.

The White Feather Experience

01 / MORNING VIEW

Greet the Sunrise Over Dudhni Lake

Wake up to gentle lakeside mist and golden sunlight coloring the surrounding forests. Greet nature at its most peaceful state.

02 / LUXURY STAY

A Sanctuary Under Premium Canvas

Step inside our fully air-conditioned glamping tents. Premium wooden structures, local decorations, and plush beds create an elegant haven.

03 / SWIMMING POOL

Dive Into Endless Emerald Waters

Float under the sky in our infinity pool. Visual borders melt into the river, creating a seamless connection with Kauncha's waterways.

04 / dining

Epicurean Lakeside Delights

Taste local specialties and fresh barbecued grills, prepared with local herbs in our scenic lakeview restaurant.

05 / NATURE WALK

Walk Paths of Forest Whispers

Explore stone-paved walkways and flower beds under forest canopies. Discover the rich flora and fauna of Dadra and Nagar Haveli.

06 / BONFIRE NIGHT

Fireside Music Under Starry Skies

As dusk falls, gather around the crackling bonfire. Enjoy soft acoustic guitar notes, local folklore, and cool lake breezes.

07 / FAMILY TIME

Cherishing Breathtaking Moments

Play indoor board games, plan lake picnics, or capture scenic family portraits at our premium photoshoot lawns.

A Taste of the Wilderness

Culinary Craftsmanship by the Lakeside

Savor the authentic flavors of the region mixed with premium global culinary arts. Dining at White Feather is designed as a sensory experience under the stars.

Our chefs use local organic herbs and fresh lake-catch fish to cook signature regional tribal delicacies. Dine on the open-air wooden deck as cool lake breezes sweep past, or organize cozy group barbecues beside the bonfire.
